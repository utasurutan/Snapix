## Overview

**What is it about?**  
Snapix is a dynamic digital ID with a built-in camera that turns live photos into retro 8-bit pixel art—press a button to snap and display your pixel avatar alongside your ID profile.

**How to do i use it?**  
1. **Power On:** Turn on Snapix using the power switch
2. **Snap a Photo:** Press the shutter button to preview the live camera, then press again to capture your portrait.
3. **Display ID:** Snapix instantly converts the photo into 8-bit pixel art and displays it next to your ID profile.
4. **Edit:** You can change your display name, profile picture and also the color of the ID background!

**Why we made it**  
Event badges and IDs are usually static, disposable, and forgettable. We wanted to build something that's a wearable hardware that's playful, unique, and a form of self-expression!
